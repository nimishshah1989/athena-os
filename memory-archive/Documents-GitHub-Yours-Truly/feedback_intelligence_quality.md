## What Nimish Rejected (2026-03-23)
- "Junior BA level" work — just basic data slicing like comparing daily averages
- Pattern detection that says "yesterday was lower than overall average" — no value
- Shallow recommendations without explaining WHY things happen
- Too many low-quality findings (153 menu findings = noise)
- No insights into what people are liking/not liking
- No utilization of all PetPooja data (consumed[], modifiers, staff_name, etc.)
- No analysis of attributes of top cafe menus nationally

## What Nimish Wants
- True actionable insights with explanations of WHY
- Deep customer insights from KOT data
- Major undercurrents and deep insights
- Fewer, higher-quality findings that are genuinely surprising
- Each insight should make the owner think "I didn't know that"
- Revenue growth focus over cost optimization
- Cultural context of Kolkata cafe scene

## Quality Bar
- After v2 rewrite with 4 engines + weekly brief + new frontend, Nimish said "this is nowhere near where we need it to be"
- The bar is VERY high — think McKinsey consultant, not data analyst
- Every finding needs: clear cause, specific action, rupee impact, evidence chain
- Deduplication and significance filtering are critical — don't flood with noise
