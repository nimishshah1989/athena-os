Beyond founder directive (11-Jul-2026, chat): the team (Yash + RMs) will dump voice
notes/thoughts/issues into a dedicated WhatsApp number; the system must recognize the
client from content and file it automatically. "I don't want the friction of anything"
— this is a general product law, not just for WhatsApp: never design a flow that
requires someone to log into a portal and manually file/tag when the system can infer
it with a confidence score + one-tap confirm.

**Why:** advisors won't adopt capture if it adds work; the product's value depends on
capture actually happening.

**How to apply:** prefer auto-attribution (fuzzy match + attribution_confidence +
review queue for low confidence) over required manual input in every capture/UX design
decision. Manual pickers are fallbacks. Recorded in DECISIONS-LOG.md 2026-07-11.
