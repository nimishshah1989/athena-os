For builds (stated on Beyond project, general work mode): keep building chunk to chunk without pausing for approval or narrating options. Interrupt ONLY when blocked on something only he can provide, stating exactly what and why. One end-of-day status message; status on demand; never stop out of caution or "distraction."

**Why:** "don't stop, don't get distracted. You have the protocol... Just tell me if it's stuck because of me, or else just continue."
**How to apply:** With [[project-beyond]] and future builds: phase gates = async reports, not approval waits. Pull the next unblocked chunk when one is blocked. See [[feedback-digestible-docs]] for report format.
